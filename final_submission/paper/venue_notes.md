# VayuDrishti: Verified Publication Venue Recommendations

## Recommended IEEE/Springer Conference Venues

### 1. IEEE International Conference on Environment and Electrical Engineering (EEEIC)
- **Scope:** Environmental monitoring, IoT, smart cities, AI for sustainability
- **Submission Format:** IEEE conference format (6–8 pages)
- **Typical Deadline:** February–March annually
- **URL:** https://eeeic.net/

### 2. IEEE International Conference on Machine Learning and Applications (ICMLA)
- **Scope:** Machine learning applications across domains including environmental science
- **Submission Format:** IEEE conference format (6–8 pages)
- **Typical Deadline:** July–August annually
- **URL:** https://www.icmla-conference.org/

### 3. Springer International Conference on Environmental Science and Technology (ICEST)
- **Scope:** Environmental AI, pollution monitoring, public health informatics
- **Submission Format:** Springer LNCS format (12–15 pages)
- **Published in:** Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) / LNEE
- **URL:** Various Springer Nature venues

### 4. IEEE International Conference on Big Data (IEEE BigData)
- **Scope:** Large-scale data processing, environmental data analytics, time-series ML
- **Submission Format:** IEEE conference format (10 pages)
- **Typical Deadline:** August–September annually
- **URL:** https://bigdataieee.org/

### 5. ACM International Conference on Information and Knowledge Management (CIKM)
- **Scope:** Applied AI, knowledge discovery, environmental informatics
- **Submission Format:** ACM format (8–10 pages)
- **Typical Deadline:** May–June annually
- **URL:** https://www.cikm.org/

## Submission Requirements
1. The team must obtain written endorsement from Prof. Soumya Sahoo before submission.
2. The manuscript `manuscript.docx` should be converted to PDF and formatted per venue-specific LaTeX/Word templates.
3. All co-authors must register institutional email affiliations.
4. Archive the submission acknowledgement email as proof of conference submission.

## Notes
- The manuscript is currently formatted in IEEE-style single-column Word format.
- For actual submission, convert to the venue's official LaTeX template (IEEE or Springer).
- The content, tables, and figures are publication-ready and can be directly transferred.
